How to get the data
  1. In Graph_Maker, pick the graph type, X, Y, group and filter.
  2. Click any cell, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C. Copying only the table with its header row works too.
  3. Click here and press Ctrl+V. The graph type is recognised from the columns.
